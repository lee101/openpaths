Provider Spotlight: DeepSeek

Provider #4

DeepSeek

DeepSeek sent shockwaves through the AI industry when they released models that matched or beat GPT-4 on key benchmarks — at roughly 95% lower cost. And they open-sourced the weights. This Chinese lab is now one of the most important forces in open AI research.

What makes DeepSeek stand out:

Genuinely open source weights

DeepSeek V3 and R1 weights are freely available. Self-host on your own hardware, fine-tune, or run via OpenPaths — no vendor lock-in.

R1: Extended reasoning at low cost

DeepSeek-R1 uses chain-of-thought reasoning like OpenAI's o-series — but at a fraction of the API cost. Excellent for math, science, and hard reasoning tasks.

V3: Frontier quality, commodity price

DeepSeek V3 benchmarks near GPT-4o and Claude Sonnet on coding and knowledge tasks — at $0.27/M input. A 10x cost advantage.

Massive parameter efficiency

DeepSeek uses Mixture of Experts (MoE) architecture — activating only a fraction of parameters per token, which is why it's so fast and cheap to run.

When should you use DeepSeek?

High-volume inference where quality matters but budget is tight. Batch processing, text analysis, code generation at scale. Use R1 when you need step-by-step reasoning. OpenPaths auto-routing will pick DeepSeek V3 when it's the right tool for the cost/quality tradeoff.
