Provider Spotlight: DeepSeek

Provider #4

DeepSeek

DeepSeek sent shockwaves through the AI industry by matching closed frontier models on key benchmarks at roughly 95% lower cost — and open-sourcing the weights. With the V4 family they've kept that lead, and this Chinese lab remains one of the most important forces in open AI research.

What makes DeepSeek stand out:

Genuinely open source weights

DeepSeek releases its model weights openly. Self-host on your own hardware, fine-tune, or run via OpenPaths — no vendor lock-in.

V4 Pro: reasoning with a 1M context

deepseek-v4-pro pairs extended chain-of-thought reasoning with a 1M token context at $1.74/M in, $3.48/M out. Excellent for math, science, and hard reasoning over large inputs.

chat & reasoner: near-frontier, commodity price

deepseek-chat and deepseek-reasoner benchmark near gpt-5.6-terra and Claude Sonnet on coding and knowledge tasks — at $0.28/M input, $0.42/M output. A 10x cost advantage.

Massive parameter efficiency

DeepSeek uses Mixture of Experts (MoE) architecture — activating only a fraction of parameters per token, which is why it's so fast and cheap to run.

When should you use DeepSeek?

High-volume inference where quality matters but budget is tight. Batch processing, text analysis, code generation at scale. Use deepseek-v4-pro when you need step-by-step reasoning or a 1M context. OpenPaths auto-routing will pick DeepSeek when it's the right tool for the cost/quality tradeoff.
