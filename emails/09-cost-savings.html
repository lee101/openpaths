Most teams overpay for AI by routing everything through one expensive model - usually gpt-5.6 or Claude Fable 5 - when 70-80% of their requests could be handled by a model that costs 10x less and delivers the same result.

The Pareto Frontier of AI

The "pareto frontier" in AI costs is the set of models where you can't get better quality without paying more, and you can't pay less without losing quality. OpenPaths auto-routing keeps you on that frontier automatically - routing each request to the optimal model based on task complexity.

The three auto-routing tiers:

auto-easy · ~$0.10-0.30/M tokens

Simple tasks: classification, summarisation, short Q&A, formatting, data extraction. Routed to gpt-5.6-luna, Gemini Flash, DeepSeek, or Haiku 4.5.

auto-medium · ~$0.50-2.00/M tokens

Standard tasks: coding, analysis, drafting, tool use. gpt-5.6-terra, Claude Sonnet 4.6, deepseek-v4-pro, or Gemini 2.5 Pro depending on complexity.

auto-think · ~$2-10/M tokens

Hard reasoning: math proofs, complex debugging, research synthesis. gpt-5.6-sol, deepseek-v4-pro, Gemini 2.5 Pro, or Claude Fable 5 with extended thinking.

How the routing works

OpenPaths uses a fast, static embedding model (runs locally in microseconds) to classify incoming requests by task type and complexity. This classification then maps to the cost-optimal model tier. No extra API call, no latency overhead.

The routing logic is open source - you can inspect exactly how your requests are classified and override at any time.
