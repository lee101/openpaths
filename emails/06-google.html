Provider Spotlight: Google DeepMind

Provider #3

Google Gemini

Google DeepMind merged two of the world's top AI labs and threw the full weight of Google's compute infrastructure behind Gemini. The result is a model family that wins on context length, multimodal reasoning, and price — especially in the Flash tier.

What makes Gemini stand out:

2 million token context window

Gemini 2.5 Pro handles 2M tokens — that's roughly 1.5 million words, an entire codebase, or hours of transcripts in a single prompt.

Best-in-class multimodal reasoning

Gemini was built multimodal from the ground up — text, images, video, audio, and code all handled natively with deep cross-modal understanding.

Gemini Flash: the price/performance king

Gemini 3.5 Flash delivers frontier-class quality at $1.50/M input with 1M context, while 2.5 Flash at $0.30/M remains the pick for high-volume production workloads.

Google Search grounding

Ground Gemini responses with live Google Search data — factual, up-to-date answers for anything that changes over time.

Gemini 2.5 Pro

2M context · deep reasoning

Complex reasoning, coding, analysis. Top-tier on coding benchmarks.

Gemini 3.5 Flash

$1.50/$9 per M · 1M context

Frontier quality at Flash pricing. The default OpenPaths auto model.

Cost perspective

Processing 100M input tokens/month with Gemini 2.5 Flash costs ~$30. The same workload on gpt-5.6 costs ~$500. That's a 16x cost difference — and Flash quality is excellent for most tasks. OpenPaths auto-routing picks Flash when the task fits, saving you money automatically.
