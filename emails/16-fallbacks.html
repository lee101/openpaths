Reliability: Automatic Fallbacks

Never Get a 503 Again

Every major AI provider has outages. OpenAI goes down around major product launches. Anthropic has rate limit windows. Google's API has regional issues. If your app talks directly to a single provider, you inherit all their availability problems.

OpenPaths sits in front of all providers and handles failures automatically.

How automatic fallbacks work:

1. Your request arrives

You send a request to openpaths.io/v1/chat/completions specifying gpt-4o or an auto tier.

2. Primary provider attempted

OpenPaths forwards to your requested provider. If it responds normally, the response comes straight back to you.

3. On error: automatic failover

On 429 (rate limit), 503 (unavailable), or timeout — OpenPaths immediately retries with the best equivalent model from another provider. Sub-second failover, no change to your response format.

4. Your app never sees the failure

From your app's perspective, the request succeeded. The response is in the same format. No retry logic needed in your code.
