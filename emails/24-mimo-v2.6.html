The pelican test

We point every new model at the same one-shot prompt: draw a pelican riding a bicycle as an animated SVG, thinking off. Flash answered with the richest scene yet at this price and the only pedaling legs. UltraSpeed wrote 863 tokens of animated wireframe. Pro drew a good pelican whose SVG was invalid XML - one duplicated attribute on a frame line, which makes browsers refuse to render the whole file. A retry returned the identical broken bytes from our response cache until the request bypassed it, which is worth knowing if you ever hit regenerate and wonder why nothing changes.

curl https://openpaths.io/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENPATHS_API_KEY" \
  -d '{"model":"xiaomi/mimo-v2.6-flash","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"say only hi"}]}'
