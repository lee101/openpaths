The Fast Inference Providers

Groq, Together AI & Fireworks

Some applications need speed above all else — real-time chat, interactive tools, latency-sensitive pipelines. A new generation of inference providers built custom silicon and optimised stacks to deliver open source models at speeds that traditional cloud GPU providers simply can't match.

Groq — LPU Inference Engine

Custom Silicon · Fastest Available

Groq built their own Language Processing Units (LPUs) specifically for transformer inference. The result: Llama 3 70B at 800+ tokens/second — 10-20x faster than GPU-based inference. No batching delays, no cold starts. If you're building real-time voice, live coding assistants, or instant-response chat, Groq is the answer.

Together AI — Open Model Platform

Broadest Open Model Catalogue

Together hosts the widest selection of open source models — Llama, Mistral, Qwen, DeepSeek, and dozens more. Strong for fine-tuning pipelines, research workflows, and production inference. Competitive pricing and a solid API with function calling support.

Fireworks AI — Production Inference

Optimized for Production · SLA-backed

Fireworks focuses on production reliability — SLA guarantees, dedicated deployments, low P99 latency. Popular with enterprises that need to self-manage open source models with commercial support. Also runs Mixtral and Llama variants exceptionally efficiently.

OpenPaths routes across all of them

When you use auto-easy or request an open source model like llama-3-70b, OpenPaths automatically selects the fastest, most cost-effective inference provider available at that moment — Groq when you need speed, Together for availability, Fireworks for production reliability. You get all three without managing three accounts.
