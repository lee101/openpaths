Provider Spotlight: Mistral AI

Provider #5

Mistral AI

Founded in Paris by former Google DeepMind and Meta researchers, Mistral has built a reputation for remarkably efficient models that punch far above their weight. They pioneered the idea that a 7B parameter model could match 70B models with the right architecture choices.

What makes Mistral stand out:

Open weights & EU data sovereignty

Mistral models are Apache 2.0 licensed. For EU companies with GDPR concerns, Mistral offers on-prem deployment with no data leaving Europe.

Codestral: built for code generation

Codestral is purpose-built for software development - fill-in-the-middle completion, 80K context, supports 80+ languages. It's the model behind several popular IDE plugins.

Fast inference with sliding window attention

Mistral's grouped-query attention and sliding window attention give it best-in-class inference speed. Low latency matters for chat and real-time applications.

Mistral Small: exceptional value

At $0.10/M input, Mistral Small 3 delivers quality that exceeds models twice its cost. One of the best cheap models available right now.
