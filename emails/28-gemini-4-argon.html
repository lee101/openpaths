Gemini 4 Argon is live: Google pricing, OpenRouter fallback

Google's Gemini 4 Argon is routable on OpenPaths today as gemini-4-argon (aliases gemini-4, google/gemini-4-argon). It bills at Google's own rates, with a 1M-token context window, tool calling and image input.

$2.00 in · $0.10 cached · $10.00 out, per million tokens

Routed direct to Google. If Google is down, requests fall back to the same model on OpenRouter at the same price, so an outage never changes your bill. The response names the model actually served.

"say hi nothing else" → "hi"

Five completion tokens, no preamble, no punctuation. Instruction-following on tiny prompts is what keeps agent loops and parsers from breaking.

The pelican-on-a-bicycle test

One shot, no retries: "Draw a pelican riding a bicycle as an animated SVG." Gemini 4 Argon returned a complete SVG with a throat pouch, cap, flowing scarf, a red bike with spoked wheels, chain, pedals and a basket, and a sea-and-road scene with CSS animation. It is the best pelican we have seen. It also took 122 seconds and 13,081 output tokens, about $0.13 at list price, so stream it and give it a generous timeout.
