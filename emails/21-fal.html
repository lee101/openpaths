Fal — Serverless generative media

Fal built a serverless runtime dedicated to generative media. No cold starts, sub-second warm calls, and new models land in their catalogue within hours of release. When you call flux-pro or flux-schnell through OpenPaths, fal is doing the heavy lifting.

FLUX — Black Forest Labs

State of the art image generation

FLUX Schnell for fast iteration ($0.003/image), FLUX Dev for higher fidelity, FLUX 1.1 Pro for production-grade renders. Same API as text — just swap the endpoint to /v1/images/generations.

FLUX 2 Klein 4B

Next-gen distilled image model

The 4B-parameter distillation of FLUX 2, available as klein. Cheap ($0.02/image), fast, and good enough for most thumbnails, hero art and asset pipelines.

Why route fal through OpenPaths?

Single API key, single balance, single bill. Usage reporting across every modality — text, image, audio, embeddings — in one dashboard. You keep the generative stack simple while we keep the providers honest.
