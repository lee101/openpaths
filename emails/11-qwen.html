Provider Spotlight: Alibaba / Qwen

Provider #7

Qwen (Alibaba)

Alibaba's Qwen series has quietly become one of the most impressive open source model families available. Qwen2.5 models match or exceed GPT-4 on many benchmarks — with exceptional multilingual capabilities, a 128K context window, and Apache 2.0 licensing. DeepSeek gets the headlines; Qwen delivers the results.

What makes Qwen stand out:

Best multilingual models available

Qwen was trained on massive Chinese and English corpora with strong coverage of Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and 29 other languages. Unmatched for East Asian language tasks.

Qwen2.5-Coder: top coding benchmark scores

The 72B Coder model scores higher than most competitors on HumanEval and other coding benchmarks. Apache-licensed and self-hostable.

QwQ: Alibaba's reasoning model

QwQ-32B is a surprisingly powerful open source reasoning model — extended chain-of-thought for math and logic, competitive with o1 at a fraction of the cost.

Apache 2.0: use commercially without restriction

Most Qwen models are Apache-licensed — fine-tune them, self-host them, build products on them. No royalties, no restrictions.
