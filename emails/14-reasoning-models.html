Temperature, top_p & reasoning effort - what actually matters

Thinking models ignore your temperature. Most reasoning modes (GPT-5.x reasoning, Claude extended thinking) fix or override sampling - leave temperature at its default and control quality with reasoning_effort (low/medium/high) or a thinking-token budget instead. That's your real cost/quality dial.

For non-thinking calls: code, math & extraction → temperature 0–0.3. Balanced chat → 0.7. Creative writing → 0.9–1.1 with top_p 0.95.

Tune one, not both. temperature rescales the whole distribution; top_p truncates its tail. Adjusting both at once makes behaviour hard to reason about - pick temperature and leave top_p alone unless you know why you need it.
