Reasoning Models: When Thinking Pays Off

o3, R1, QwQ — The Thinking Models

A new class of AI model arrived in 2024: models that "think" before they answer. They generate an internal chain of reasoning — sometimes thousands of tokens — before producing a final response. The results are remarkable for hard problems, but the cost is real.

When is extended thinking worth it?

✅ USE thinking models

Complex math proofs
Multi-step logical reasoning
Competitive programming
Scientific problem-solving
Debugging mysterious bugs
Strategy & planning tasks

❌ Skip thinking models

Simple Q&A
Text summarisation
Data extraction
Content generation
Chatbot responses
High-volume classification

The thinking models available via OpenPaths:

OpenAI o3

The benchmark king. Top scores on AIME math, Codeforces, GPQA. Use when correctness is non-negotiable and cost is secondary.

~$10/M input

OpenAI o4-mini

Near-o3 reasoning at 80% lower cost. The best ROI in the reasoning model category for most use cases.

~$1.10/M

DeepSeek R1

Open source extended reasoning. Open weights, self-hostable, surprisingly competitive with o1. ~95% cheaper than o3 via API.

~$0.55/M

QwQ-32B

Alibaba's open reasoning model. Apache-licensed, self-hostable, strong on math and logic. The cheapest entry into reasoning model territory.

~$0.40/M
