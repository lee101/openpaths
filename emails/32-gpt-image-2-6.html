OpenAI's newest image models, $0.06 an image

Four new OpenAI image models are routable on OpenPaths today: gpt-image-2.6-sunburst, gpt-image-2.6-flare, gpt-image-2.5-sunburst and gpt-image-2.5-flare. They generate and edit through the same /v1/images/generations and /v1/images/edits endpoints as every other image model, at $0.06 per 1K image.

Same prompt, all four models

We ran the same four prompts through each model at high quality and kept every result, good or not. Left to right: 2.6 Sunburst, 2.6 Flare, 2.5 Sunburst, 2.5 Flare.

Prompt: "Minimalist poster for a jazz night titled MIDNIGHT ROUTES, bold typography, deep blue and gold, saxophone silhouette, small text at the bottom: Fri 9pm, Blue Door Club". 1024x1536. All four spelled the title and the small print correctly.
Prompt: "A beige ceramic coffee mug on a wooden table, soft natural window light, editorial product photo, with the handwritten text 'OPEN PATHS' on a chalkboard behind it". 1024x1024.
Prompt: a four-step infographic titled "How an API gateway routes a request". 1536x1024. All four got the title and the four step labels right; 2.5 Flare packed in the most small print, which is where text drifts, so proofread it.
Edit: one reference photo in, "Change the label text to say OPENPATHS and make the liquid emerald green". Scene, bottle shape and flowers preserved by every model. Sent to /v1/images/edits with image_url.

It is not magic: the same prompt gives four different compositions, so pick by looking, not by version number. Dense infographic body text still needs a proofread, and sunburst runs can take 30 to 45 seconds when many are in flight.

Sunburst · $0.06 / image

2.6 and 2.5. The slower tier, about 25 to 45 seconds per 1K image in our runs. Reach for it on posters, hero shots and layouts where composition matters.

Flare · $0.06 / image

2.6 and 2.5. The fast tier: 2.6 Flare finished in 9 to 15 seconds. Good for iteration, bulk variations and anything interactive.

Pricing and fallback

$0.06 per image at 1024 sizes, $0.11 at 2048 and 4K, plus $0.01 per input image on edits. If OpenAI is unavailable the request falls back to GPT Image 2 automatically.

curl https://openpaths.io/v1/images/generations \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENPATHS_API_KEY" \
  -d '{"model":"gpt-image-2.6-flare","prompt":"Poster titled MIDNIGHT ROUTES, deep blue and gold","size":"1024x1536","quality":"high"}'
