ManifoldGen video + image upscale

ManifoldGen's video generator and image upscaler are now routable on OpenPaths, at the same prices as manifoldgen.com. Use your existing API key.

kfold-video: $0.15 per second

Cinematic video with native audio from ManifoldGen's K-Fold/H3 lanes. Text-to-video and image-to-video through the standard /v1/videos routes. Also routable: wan-animate character animation, h3-control-video restyle and video-dramatize.

image-upscale: $0.02 (2x) and image-upscale-4x: $0.03

Faithful upscaling of a source image, sent as image_url to /v1/images/generations. image-upscale-creative ($0.15) redraws fine detail instead of just enlarging.

curl https://openpaths.io/v1/images/generations \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENPATHS_API_KEY" \
  -d '{"model":"image-upscale-4x","image_url":"https://example.com/photo.jpg"}'
