Kimi K3.1 Pro direct and Kling 4.0 video

Two launches: Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro and K3 are routed direct at list price, and Kling 4.0 video (text, image, reference and edit) is live at fal's published rates.

Kimi K3.1 Pro, K3 and Highspeed

kimi-k3.1-pro, kimi-k3 and kimi-k3.1-pro-highspeed go straight to Moonshot with 0% markup, a 1M-token context window, tool calling and image input. Cache hits bill at the discounted rate, and if Moonshot is down requests fall back to the same model on OpenRouter (or Alibaba Cloud for K3).

K3.1 Pro: $2.00 in · $0.20 cached · $10.00 out, per million tokens

K3: $3.00 / $0.30 / $15.00. K3.1 Pro Highspeed: $4.00 / $0.20 / $20.00 for faster output.

curl https://openpaths.io/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENPATHS_API_KEY" \
  -d '{"model":"kimi-k3.1-pro","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"say hi nothing else"}]}'

Kling 4.0 video

Kling 4.0 makes 3 to 30 second clips at 720p or 1080p. Four routes, billed per second of output at fal's published rates:

Text and image to video: $0.157/s at 720p · $0.235/s at 1080p

kling-4.0-text-to-video and kling-4.0-image-to-video. Image-to-video takes a start frame and an optional end frame. Aspect ratios 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 21:9.

Reference to video: same rates, or $0.188/s · $0.282/s with video references

kling-4.0-reference-to-video takes up to 10 images and 5 videos as @Image1 / @Video1 references in the prompt. Video references bill on input plus output length, at least twice the output.

Video edit: $0.188/s at 720p · $0.282/s at 1080p

kling-4.0-video-edit rewrites a 3 to 30 second source video from a text instruction, such as “change the background to a tropical island at sunset”, with optional image references.

curl https://openpaths.io/v1/videos/generations \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENPATHS_API_KEY" \
  -d '{"model":"kling-4.0-text-to-video","prompt":"A detective on a rainy rooftop looks through a telescope","duration":5,"resolution":"720p"}'
