OpenPaths

Game concept art for 3.4 cents an image

Two Google image models are routable on OpenPaths today: gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image (Nano Banana 2 Lite) and gemini-nano-banana-2.1. Both generate and edit images at $0.0336 per 1K image through the same /v1/images/generations and /v1/images/edits endpoints as every other image model.

What it means for game dev

A concept screenshot is the expensive artifact. Every variation of it - another time of day, another season, a damaged state, a new UI screen - used to be a paint-over. We took one castle-builder mockup and sent it plus one sentence per variation to the edits endpoint. Nine images, about thirty cents.

The source: one castle-builder concept screenshot
Night siege - same camera, layout and HUD, new objective banner
Deep winter - year and objective updated in the HUD
Dragon aftermath - a damaged state for the mood board
A battle-mode screen that does not exist yet, in the same HUD style
Build-menu icon sheet in the game's art style

It is not magic: pixel-art redraws of a screenshot mostly kept the original render, the battle-mode UI text is plausible rather than exact, and the icon sheet came back with fourteen cells instead of the twelve we asked for. Count your cells and keep what you need.

gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image · $0.0336 / image

Nano Banana 2 Lite: the cheap, fast one. 8-18 seconds per image in our runs. Good for icon sheets, key art and bulk variations.

gemini-nano-banana-2.1 · $0.0336 / image

Higher fidelity and better multi-turn consistency at the same price. 23-25 seconds in our runs. Reach for it on hero shots and UI studies.

Also added: gemini-3-flash-preview · $0.50 in / $3.00 out

The previous Flash model, 1M context, same price at every context length. gemini-3.1-flash-lite stays at $0.25 / $1.50.

Image edits now fall back to Gemini

openpaths/image-edit tries Nano Banana 2 Lite and 2.1 before the other edit providers, so an edit does not fail because one provider is down.

curl https://openpaths.io/v1/images/edits \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENPATHS_API_KEY" \
  -d '{"model":"gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image","prompt":"Same game, deep winter. Keep layout and HUD.","image_url":"https://example.com/screenshot.png"}'
