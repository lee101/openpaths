Claude Haiku 5.5 is live: $0.10 per million input tokens

Anthropic's newest Haiku is routable on OpenPaths today as claude-haiku-5-5 (alias claude-haiku-latest). It bills at Anthropic's own rates: $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens for prompts up to 100K tokens, with a 1M context window and 128K max output.

The tiers, in one place

Up to 100K-token prompts · $0.10 in / $0.50 out

Cached input is $0.01 per million, 90% off. This is the rate almost every chat, support and extraction request lands on.

Over 100K-token prompts · $0.50 in / $2.50 out

The whole request moves to the higher rate card once the prompt crosses 100K tokens, so long-document jobs cost 5x more per token.

Effort levels · low, medium, high, xhigh, max

Send reasoning_effort on the OpenAI endpoint or output_config.effort on the Anthropic one. We tested "say hi nothing else" at every level and got "Hi" back each time. "none" turns thinking off.

Haiku 5.5 vs DeepSeek V4.1 Flash

We built a pricing calculator that answers exactly this kind of question with the tiers included. Across ten realistic workloads Haiku 5.5 is cheaper in nine: a 5M-request classification job costs $225 a month on Haiku against $399 on DeepSeek Flash. DeepSeek wins the tenth, 150K-token document analysis, at $147 against $413, because Haiku's long-context tier kicks in. DeepSeek is also cheaper off-peak ($0.15 in / $0.60 out) than at peak ($0.30 / $1.20).

On Artificial Analysis, Haiku 5.5 scores 43.4 on the Intelligence Index against 39.5 for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, and runs at about 243 output tokens per second against 216.

Worth knowing before you switch

Haiku 5.5 uses a newer tokenizer that produces roughly 30% more tokens than Haiku 4.5 for the same text, so compare cost per task. Sampling parameters like temperature and top_p are not forwarded to this model. Claude Haiku 4.5 stays available.

curl https://openpaths.io/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENPATHS_API_KEY" \
  -d '{"model":"claude-haiku-5-5","reasoning_effort":"low","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"say hi nothing else"}]}'
